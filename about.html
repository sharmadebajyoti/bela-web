§01 — The Studio

Small studio,
large obsession.

Bela-Web is an independent web design practice. We work with a handful of clients each year — long enough to do the thing properly, small enough to stay obsessed with the details.

Most of our work begins with a conversation about the brand, the people behind it, and the gap between where the website is and where it should be. Then we close that gap.

§02 — How we work

A four-act play.

Act I

Discover

Conversations, audits, immersion. We listen before we draw.

Act II

Define

Strategy, sitemap, brand language. The map before the journey.

Act III

Design

Art direction, layouts, motion, prototype. The performance.

Act IV

Deploy

Build, test, launch — and keep watching after the curtain.
